# UNSC Reborn

A Halo total conversion for **Starsector 0.98a-RC8**: two full navies built at
canonical scale, a Covenant campaign with its own rank ladder, ONI's three
Sections, an excavation under a Covenant forge world, and the door at the
bottom of it that should have stayed shut.

A rebuild of **AppleMarineXX's** UNSC mod - the faction, the lore and most of
the art are theirs.

## Download

**[Download the latest release](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1T_90GfQFG-2wWWEHYPlAKMf_5rXq_Sw3)** (Google Drive)

Requires [LazyLib](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=5444.0)
and [MagicLib](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=13718.0).
Nexerelin supported. LunaLib optional - it puts the campaign's pacing on
in-game sliders.

Drop the `UNSC` folder into `Starsector/mods`, enable it with the two
libraries, and start a new game. Not save-compatible across major versions,
and not compatible with the original UNSC mod (both define the same ids).

- **Forum thread:** https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35803.0

## What's in it

- **Two full navies** - 36 UNSC and 29 Covenant warships, every hull sized
  from its canonical length and armed from its canonical armament table
- **37 weapons, no vanilla blueprints** - the MAC line, Archers, Shivas and
  Ramparts; plasma torpedoes, pulse lasers, needlers and energy projectors
- **Two careers** - the Covenant Hierarchy's seven grades earned in devotion,
  and ONI's twelve-grade ladder paid in requisition chits with red, black and
  omega clearances earned by deniable work
- **A front line that moves** - suppression forces gather, cross the sector
  under their own power, and burn worlds nobody saves
- **The excavation** - a shaft under a Covenant world, two years deep, that
  both careers can push, slow, or go down, with three endings
- **The Flood** - nine infested hulls, three of them human and still carrying
  their own spinal guns, and a ship system that knits hull back to eighty
  percent and never to whole

## This repository

This repo hosts the mod's **Version Checker** data. `UNSC_Reborn.version` is
read by [Version Checker](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=8181.0)
to announce updates in-game; its `modVersion` block is bumped on each release.
